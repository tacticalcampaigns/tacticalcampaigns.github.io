How we work

Official sources first

State and county figures are the anchor. County sums must equal statewide totals — every panel reconciles, or it doesn't ship.

Compare like-for-like

Comparison years are cut at the same days before Election Day, never the same calendar date.

Projections wear a label

Forecasts are marked experimental, published with their backtests, and never dressed up as results.
